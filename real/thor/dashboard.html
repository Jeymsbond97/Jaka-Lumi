Who is speaking (voice direction)

The six microphones in the head hear where a voice comes from. To match that with the cameras, calibrate once: press Start, then stand alone, 1–1.5 m in front of the robot and talk for a few seconds; step about 1 m to the left and talk again, then to the right. 6–8 samples are enough.
