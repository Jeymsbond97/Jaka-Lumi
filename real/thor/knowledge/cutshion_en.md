## Company

Cutshion Co., Ltd. (in Korean 주식회사 쿳션) is a Korean robot company. It was founded as a company in July 2022. The CEO is Lee Min-seok (이민석); Cutshion is his third company. Its slogan is "Soft & Flexible Idea", and its mission is to be "The Best Robot Application Provider": not only to build and sell robots, but to create robot applications and an ecosystem for them.

What Cutshion does: it develops and supplies smart factory solutions based on AI vision, collaborative robots (cobots) and autonomous mobile robots (AMR). It builds robot apps (barista, AI drawing, welding, palletizing, cooking, ice cream, candy ball and educational robots), its own technology (CUVision AI vision, the Pionoid robot development platform, the GOMCOOK cooking robot platform), and it sells robot hardware from partner makers and SOLIDWORKS design software. Its goal is to become a robot platform service provider whose platform works on any robot.

Cutshion is a young startup (founded in 2022, a certified venture business). Cutshion milestones: venture business certification (December 2023), its own corporate research lab (July 2023), a seed investment of 500 million won from CNT Tech and Evergreen Investment Partners (August 2025), and selection for the TIPS Global Track program (December 2025).

Cutshion awards: RoboWorld Award 2023 in robot services for GOMCOOK, RoboWorld Award 2024 in manufacturing robots for the mobile welding robot, the Excellence Award at the Venturium Demo Day (May 2026), and the VivaTech Tech for Change Award in Paris (June 2026).

Cutshion exhibitions: RoboWorld in Korea (2022, 2023), IFA Berlin (2023), Gangnam Robot Plus Festival (2023, 2024), Smart Life Week (2024), Korea ICT Expo in Japan (2024), CES in Las Vegas (2025 and 2026), the NRA Show in Chicago (2026) and VivaTech in Paris (2026).

Cutshion partners listed on its website include OMRON, Zimmer Group Korea, Screen HD Korea, Daesung Industrial, BorgWarner, THIRA Robotics, Calmantech, Sinsung F.A, Hyunwoo Automation, Deeplant, BizData, Ikseon 121, WithinNet, Cho & Company, Oldmug, Cake Drama and Bogate.

## Products and robots

Cutshion's robot apps: barista robot, AI drawing robot, welding robot, palletizing robot, cooking robot, ice cream robot, candy ball robot and an educational robot kit. Its technology: CUVision (AI vision), Pionoid (robot development platform) and GOMCOOK (cooking robot platform). It also sells collaborative robots, TUSK logistics robots, PEITIAN industrial robots and SOLIDWORKS design software.

Cutshion's barista robot makes espresso coffee fully automatically. Unlike vending coffee robots, it runs a real espresso machine and does the barista's main steps itself: grinding the beans, dosing, tamping and extracting. It is an all-in-one barista station that keeps the same taste every time, serves fast and reliably and lowers staff costs. It works together with human baristas, taking over the repeated steps. In December 2025 Cutshion signed a yearly barista robot supply agreement with Mega Coffee. In September 2026 news reported a deal (contract) to supply 200 barista robots to Sonovictus in the USA (the US deal), delivered over three years from 2027. Its price is not on the website, so Lumi does not know it.

Cutshion's AI drawing robot takes your photo with a camera, AI turns it into a line drawing, and the robot arm draws it on a canvas. One button starts the selfie and the drawing. It can be mobile or fixed, comes as an all-in-one type or a kiosk type, and a brand can be printed on the photo backdrop, the arm and the canvas, so it is popular for promotion at events and in education. Drawing robots were supplied to the Gyeongnam Office of Education (2024) and two to the Jeonbuk Office of Education's science bus (2024). Its price is not on the website, so Lumi does not know it.

Cutshion's cooking robot is an AI cooking robot that supports personalised cooking for each customer. It comes with Cutshion's own vision solution and the GOMCOOK platform, keeps the taste and quality the same, and lowers staff and ingredient costs. Cutshion supplied a chicken-and-beer (chimaek) cooking robot to the Itaewon 121 X Barney & Carrot restaurant in Seoul (2024) and signed a chicken robot contract with Bogate in India (2024). Its price is not on the website, so Lumi does not know it.

Cutshion's ice cream robot serves soft ice cream: the customer orders on a touch-screen kiosk and the robot makes the ice cream and hands it over, with no staff needed. Model SOFTICE001: about 30 seconds per ice cream, holds 40 cones, weighs 500 kg, size 140 x 140 x 165 cm, 220 V. It suits franchise ice cream brands, shops and events. Its price is not on the website, so Lumi does not know it.

Cutshion's candy ball robot is a fun event robot: you choose a colour on the touch screen and the robot picks a candy ball of that colour and gives it to you. Model CANDYBALL001: about 20 seconds per candy ball (that is how fast it is), weighs 273 kg, size 1.3 x 1.1 x 1.9 m, 220 V, plug and play for short events. Candy ball robots were supplied to the Taean Office of Education (2024). Its price is not on the website, so Lumi does not know it.

Cutshion's welding robot is a lightweight collaborative welding robot. It does multi-layer, multi-pass arc welding, follows the weld seam automatically, works with many kinds of welders, is taught by dragging the arm or with graphical programming, and is easy to move between work places. The mobile welding robot won the RoboWorld Award 2024. Its price is not on the website, so Lumi does not know it.

Cutshion's palletizing robot stacks finished products on pallets automatically and can run 24 hours. Made with Screen, a global printing equipment company, it is Korea's first palletizing robot made for book publishing. It handles up to 20 kg, one cycle takes about 15 seconds, items from B5 to A4 size, and it saves workers from heavy lifting. Its price is not on the website, so Lumi does not know it.

Cutshion's educational robot is an all-in-one robot training kit in one portable flight case: a small collaborative robot arm, practice modules (vision, conveyor, drawing, assembly, glue coating, puzzle, handling and more), digital twin simulation software and course materials. It is used by schools, universities, research labs and companies. Its arm carries 1 kg and reaches 58 cm. Its price is not on the website, so Lumi does not know it.

Cutshion's CUVision is its AI vision technology: computers understanding camera images. It analyses the surface of work pieces, tracks 3D movement after calibration and uses deep learning to recognise paths and surfaces, for example for quality checks in smart factories. Cutshion developed an AI vision sensor in 2023.

Cutshion's Pionoid is its robot development platform: one unified robot API to control robots from any maker, so the same robot app runs on different robots. It is built on webROS, made by Cutshion, and works like an app store where robot apps are shared and downloaded (Pionoid Factory to build apps, Pionoid Store to share them). Cooking, drawing and palletizing apps run on it.

Cutshion's GOMCOOK is its cooking robot platform for personalised cooking. It connects to delivery and ordering apps through an open API, covering order, payment, cooking and delivery in one service. A patent has been applied for, and GOMCOOK won the RoboWorld Award 2023.

TUSK robots (sold by Cutshion): TUSK logistics robots are autonomous pallet-handling robots for factories and warehouses; they lift up to 1,000 kg and connect to warehouse systems (WMS, MES, ERP).

PEITIAN robots (sold by Cutshion): PEITIAN industrial robots carry payloads from 3 to 280 kg. In May 2026 Cutshion signed a partnership with PEITIAN Robotics. Cutshion also sells collaborative robots (cobots) from a partner maker.

Cutshion's robot design service: it offers SOLIDWORKS 3D design software (design, simulation, data management, electrical design, flow simulation, DraftSight 2D CAD), helps customers choose the right SOLIDWORKS products, and designs and builds robot and automation systems itself.

## 한국어: 쿳션

쿳션(주식회사 쿳션, Cutshion)은 로봇 애플리케이션을 만드는 한국 로봇 회사예요. 2022년 7월에 법인을 설립한 젊은 스타트업(벤처기업 인증)이고, 대표이사는 이민석 대표님이에요. 슬로건은 'Soft & Flexible Idea'이고, 로봇을 만들고 파는 것을 넘어 로봇 애플리케이션 생태계를 만드는 'The Best Robot Application Provider'가 목표예요. AI 비전 기술을 바탕으로 협동로봇과 AMR을 활용한 스마트 팩토리 솔루션을 개발해 공급해요.

쿳션의 로봇 앱은 바리스타 로봇, AI 드로잉 로봇, 용접 로봇, 팔레타이징 로봇, 조리 로봇, 아이스크림 로봇, 캔디볼 로봇, 교육용 로봇이에요. 자체 기술로 AI 비전 큐비전(CUVision), 로봇 개발 플랫폼 피오노이드(Pionoid), 조리 로봇 플랫폼 곰쿡(GOMCOOK)이 있어요. 협동로봇, 물류로봇(TUSK), 산업로봇(PEITIAN) 같은 로봇 하드웨어와 솔리드웍스 설계 소프트웨어도 공급해요.

쿳션의 바리스타 로봇은 에스프레소 머신을 직접 제어해서 원두 분쇄, 도징, 탬핑, 추출까지 바리스타의 핵심 작업을 완전히 자동으로 해요. 언제나 같은 맛을 내고 인건비 부담을 줄여 줘요. 2025년 12월 메가커피와 바리스타 로봇 연간 공급 계약을 맺었고, 2026년 9월에는 미국 소노빅터스에 바리스타 로봇 200대를 3년에 걸쳐 공급하는 계약 소식이 있었어요. 가격은 홈페이지에 없어서 몰라요.

쿳션의 AI 드로잉 로봇은 카메라로 찍은 사진을 AI가 라인 드로잉으로 바꾸고, 로봇 팔이 캔버스에 그림을 그려 줘요. 버튼 하나로 촬영과 그리기가 되고, 일체형과 키오스크형이 있어요. 배경, 로봇 팔, 캔버스에 브랜드를 인쇄할 수 있어서 행사와 교육에서 인기가 많아요. 가격은 홈페이지에 없어서 몰라요.

쿳션의 조리 로봇은 개인 맞춤 조리를 지원하는 AI 조리 로봇이고, 조리 로봇 플랫폼 곰쿡과 함께 써요. 같은 맛과 품질을 지키고 인건비와 식재료 비용을 줄여 줘요. 이태원 매장에 치맥 로봇을 공급했어요. 가격은 홈페이지에 없어서 몰라요.

쿳션의 아이스크림 로봇은 키오스크 주문부터 아이스크림 추출, 전달까지 무인으로 해요. 한 개에 약 30초가 걸리고 콘을 40개 넣을 수 있어요. 가격은 홈페이지에 없어서 몰라요.

쿳션의 캔디볼 로봇은 터치스크린에서 고른 색의 캔디볼을 로봇이 뽑아서 전달하는 체험형 로봇이에요. 한 번에 약 20초가 걸리고 행사에 잘 맞아요. 가격은 홈페이지에 없어서 몰라요.

쿳션의 용접 로봇은 가벼운 협동 용접 로봇이에요. 다층 다중 패스 아크 용접과 용접선 자동 추적을 하고, 여러 용접기와 호환돼요. 모바일 용접 로봇으로 로보월드 어워드 2024를 받았어요. 가격은 홈페이지에 없어서 몰라요.

쿳션의 팔레타이징 로봇은 완성품을 팔레트에 자동으로 쌓는 로봇이고 24시간 운영할 수 있어요. 스크린사와 함께 만든 국내 최초 출판 전용 팔레타이징 로봇이에요. 가격은 홈페이지에 없어서 몰라요.

쿳션의 교육용 로봇은 협동로봇, 실습 모듈, 디지털 트윈 소프트웨어, 교육 자료를 휴대용 케이스 하나에 담은 올인원 실험 키트예요. 학교, 대학, 연구소, 기업 교육에 써요. 가격은 홈페이지에 없어서 몰라요.

쿳션의 큐비전은 카메라 영상을 AI로 이해하는 AI 비전 기술이에요. 피오노이드는 제조사와 상관없이 하나의 통합 로봇 API로 로봇 앱을 만들 수 있는 로봇 개발 플랫폼이고, 곰쿡은 배달·주문 앱과 연동되는 조리 로봇 플랫폼이에요. 솔리드웍스 설계 소프트웨어도 공급하고, TUSK 물류로봇과 PEITIAN 산업용 로봇도 판매해요.

쿳션이 받은 상(수상): 로보월드 어워드 2023(곰쿡), 로보월드 어워드 2024(모바일 용접 로봇), 2026 벤처리움 데모데이 우수상, 2026 비바테크 Tech for Change Award. CES 2025, CES 2026, 2026 NRA 시카고 전시회에도 참가했어요.
