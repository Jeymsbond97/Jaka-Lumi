# 14. Voice assistant: required behaviour

How Lumi must behave when it talks with people. The requirements come from the product owner. Each one is marked with how it is implemented (`real/thor/assistant/lumi.py`, `assistant/brain.py`, `audio/ears.py`, `audio/mic.py`) and whether it has been tested on the robot. Change the code to match this page, not the other way round. When a requirement changes, update this page first.

Status: ✅ tested on the robot · 🟡 implemented, not yet tested live · ⬜ not implemented

---

## 1. Languages

| Requirement | Implementation | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Understand and answer **English and Korean** | whisper.cpp (`large-v3-turbo`, auto language) → Ollama `gemma4:26b` → Supertonic TTS (voice F1) | ✅ |
| **Answer in the language the person just used**, switching at once (EN ↔ KO) | `ears.last_stt["language"]` → `brain.FORCED_LANG` → system prompt names the answer language | 🟡 |
| "English로 말해 줄래요?", "영어로 자기소개 해 봐", "talk in Korean": answered in the language asked for; in English the company is always "Cutshion" | `brain.reply_language`, `brain.spoken` | 🟡 |
| Other languages are not answered | only `english` / `korean` count as understood | ✅ |
| Another language in Latin letters typed or misheard as text ("menga suv ber") → "Sorry, I only understand English and Korean." | `brain.other_latin_language` (one short LLM call on an unclear action) | 🟡 |

## 2. Waking and finding the caller

| Requirement | Implementation | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Wake words: **"Lumi", "Hi", "Hi Lumi", "Hey Lumi"** (Korean "루미야") from anywhere, also 3–4 m away and from behind | `WAKE`, `HELLO_ONLY` in `lumi.py`; wireless clip mic (DJI Mic Mini) when connected, else the head mic array | ✅ (near), 🟡 (3–4 m) |
| A clap, a thud, music or a laugh never wakes Lumi: whisper writes its prompt's "Lumi." for such sounds (13 of 15 "Lumi." in the heard clips of 2026-10-21 were no speech) | a text that is only the name ("Lumi.", "Hey Lumi.", "루미야.") is transcribed once more without the prompt and dropped when no word there sounds like the name (`ears.CALL_ONLY`, `ears.lumi_like`) | 🟡 |
| Music, hum or noise with nobody speaking is never a turn: whisper writes "-", "-끝-", "감사합니다.", "네.", "자막 제공 및 광고를 포함하고 있습니다." for soft music (2026-10-22: they became goodbyes and "say it again" inside a talk). A sound with under 0.1 s of speech frames is not sent to whisper; a text with no word, starting with a subtitle dash or a caption credit, or with more syllables than the speech held (0.05 s each) is dropped; the log says why ("heard nothing (..., no speech (0.00 s): music or noise)"). Sung words in a song with vocals still pass (they are speech) | `ears.speech_seconds()` (Silero VAD, `models/silero_vad.onnx`, local only), `MIN_SPEECH_S`, `VAD_P`, `SPEECH_PER_SYLLABLE`, `NOT_SAID`; `tests/asr_check.py --cases 4` | 🟡 |
| Find **who** called: the voice direction (6-mic array, SRP-PHAT, self-calibrating) matched to a person in the camera | `doa.py`, `find_caller()` | ✅ |
| Caller **outside the camera view**: the head camera turns the head first (sees ±102°: 42° + head ±60°); farther, turn the base toward the voice, then look for the person | `CAMERA_HALF_FOV` 42°, `head.YAW_LIMIT`, `agv.turn_by` | 🟡 |
| Reply to a bare wake word: "Yes? How can I help?" | `WAKE_REPLY` | 🟡 |
| A command spoken together with the wake word ("Hi Lumi, come here") is done at once | `check_wake()` | ✅ |

## 4. Ending a conversation

| Requirement | Implementation | Status |
|---|---|---|
| Understanding (**"I see", "got it", "okay I see", "makes sense", "understood", "아 그렇구나", "알겠어요", "그렇군요", "아하", "네 알겠습니다"**, mixed "oh 그렇구나") → a short acknowledgement or one related point by the LLM; the talk stays open. While Lumi talks, these and "uh huh" don't interrupt it | `brain.UNDERSTOOD`, `brain.is_backchannel`, `lumi.talk_fate` → "talk", the prompt | 🟡 |
| A check (**"I see?", "really?", "is that so?", "그래요?", "정말?", "그런가요?", "그렇구나?"**) → Lumi says yes and repeats its last point in one short sentence | `brain.is_backchannel` (no lookup, no action), the prompt | 🟡 |
| Words that close a talk in one context and mean "understood, go on" in another (**"okay", "thank you", "that's it", "got it, thanks", "됐어요", "오케이 됐어", "네 감사합니다", "땡큐"**) → the conversation model decides with the turns before: its action end is the goodbye (its own words); otherwise it answers ("You're welcome.") and `CLOSING_WAIT_S` (8 s) of silence ends the talk. ## 5. Interrupting Lumi (barge-in)

| Requirement | Implementation | Status |
|---|---|---|
| While Lumi speaks, "stop", "okay", "thanks", "got it" stop the speech | clip mic keeps listening while Lumi talks (`mic.BARGE_IN`), `barge_in()` | ✅ |
| A new question interrupts the answer and is answered | `pending_text` | ✅ |
| Lumi's own voice must not count as an interruption | ignored when ≥ 60 % of the words match what Lumi said in the last 30 s, or when the text is unclear (log-probability < −0.6 or in the wrong script) | 🟡 |
| Words said right after Lumi stops are the visitor's, even when they repeat Lumi's words ("Can you see me?" after "I can't see you", "Lumi." after the greeting were dropped ~20 times on 2026-10-21) | clip mic: the 60 % word match counts only for an utterance recorded while Lumi talked (`mic.Utterance.during_speech`); head array: only for 1–2 words (`lumi.talk_turn`) | 🟡 |

Keep the clip mic's transmitter on the visitor, not on the robot: taped near the head it hears the speaker and interrupts Lumi.

## 6. Motion commands by voice

| Command | Action | Status |
|---|---|---|
| "Come here", "come to me", "이리 와" | drive to a free spot ~1 m in front of the caller (`nav.py`), stop at 1.1 m | ✅ |
| "Go to p2", "go home", "go to the charger" | drive to a map marker | ✅ |
| "Follow me", "come with me", "walk with me", "따라와", "나 따라와", "같이 가자" | `tasks/follow.py`: checked before "Okay": on the charger, or the arm off / not answering and not already in `look` ("My arm is switched off, so I can't drive safely. Arm to `look`, waist front, the head stays on the person (ahead they may be out of view), then the base follows the person who said it ~1 m behind, smoothly at their walking speed up to 0.5 m/s (the base's limit), slowing down to stop at the gap, turning toward them; between camera sightings it drives toward where they should be by now (no stop-and-go when frames are missed). The person is kept by place: a track that jumps to a passer-by is not taken, the person's own new track near where they should be is (also when lost), not when its clothes are clearly different. Something in the way: the base stands and goes on when it clears. Ends on any stop word ("stop", "wait", "enough", "that's enough", "stay here", "멈춰", "그만", "거기 있어", "기다려"; also while Lumi talks), "I lost you, so I stopped. Simple commands are matched by rules (`brain.INTENTS`) and done without waiting for the LLM; everything else goes through the LLM's `ACTION:` line, which lists every ability with its parameters and limits (`brain.ACTIONS_STREAM`) and maps any wording to one of them ("Eyes on me, please." → look me, "Spin around." → turn left 360); code checks the action and its values (`Brain._action`). What the body can't do (jump, dance, fly, stairs, doors) is answered plainly. Body commands in any wording are checked by `tests/dialogue_check.py --commands`.

Pick and place run in the background (up to a minute) while Lumi keeps listening, so "stop" works during them; no wave is started meanwhile. Objects are the camera classes cup, bottle, apple, orange, banana (spoken "mug", "glass" mean cup). A colour in the request must match the colour the camera measures, which changes with the light (a white cup can read "grey"): without a colour the nearest such object is taken. Reasons are spoken in English; in Korean Lumi says only that it cannot do it now.

## 8. Tuning values

| Value | Where | Now |
|---|---|---|
| Speech start threshold | `mic.START_FACTOR`, `MIN_LEVEL`, `MIN_LEVEL_CLIP` | 1.8 × noise, 250 / 300 RMS |

| Speaking speed | `data/speech_speed.txt`, "speak slower" / "faster" (`voice_words.SPEED_STEP`, `mouth.SPEED_MIN` / `MAX`) | 0.92 (`mouth.SPEED`) |

| LLM prompt cache | `brain.warm` / `llm_direct.prefill` | before each listen the routing prompt's start (system + turns kept) is sent to the runner, so a question costs only its last message (~0.5 s), also after a lookup or a mission read-back |
